---
name: component-research
description: Research electronic component prices, stock, substitutes and distributor offers online for delivery to Portugal.
---

1. Establish the exact manufacturer part number or required specifications and quantity. Ask for quantity if missing. Flag ambiguous matches before comparing offers.
2. Call web_search with the part, quantity and delivery to Portugal. Prefer manufacturer and distributor product pages. Cross-check competing offers and open specific product URLs through additional web_search queries when the initial evidence is only snippets.
3. Compare available offers in EUR. For each offer give the exact part, vendor, unit price and quantity tier, stock, VAT status, shipping and a source link. Mark unverified or unavailable fields explicitly. Keep foreign-currency offers in their source currency unless a sourced exchange rate is available.
4. Recommend an offer only when its exact match and relevant price tier are evidenced. Distinguish the component subtotal from landed cost; missing shipping or VAT means the landed total is unknown. Include the research date and links for every claimed price/stock figure.

Complete when the user has sourced offers or a precise account of what could not be verified. Search snippets are leads, not confirmed checkout prices. Research does not place orders.
